# AutoTube Studio: retention plan for all 66 styles

Goal: as many people as possible watch to the end. A realistic target is **50-70% average view duration on a 5-minute video**, and **80%+ "viewed" (not swiped away) on Shorts**, with loops pushing Shorts above 100%. Nobody can guarantee a number. These rules move it.

## 1. Retention rules every video follows
1. **Hook in 0-3 s.** Open on the most surprising claim, never on a logo or a "hello". (*"In 1930 the most powerful empire on Earth was beaten by something in your kitchen."*)
2. **Open loop in 15 s.** Promise a payoff that only comes at the end. (*"The craziest part happens on a quiet beach…"*)
3. **The picture changes every 3-5 s.** Each narration line is cut into 1-3 shots on exact words. No shot runs over 7 s.
4. **Word-by-word captions.** Big type with the spoken word highlighted. Many people watch muted.
5. **A pattern interrupt every 30-45 s:** a new chapter style, a sting, a countdown number, a silent beat or a "punch" card.
6. **A cliffhanger at the end of each chapter**, then a visible chapter tracker (5 bars at the top) so viewers know how much is left.
7. **Sound design:** a whoosh on each cut, impacts on reveals, typewriter, stamp, radar beeps, a projector. The music mood changes with each chapter and ducks under the voice.
8. **Payoff and loop.** The ending answers the hook. Shorts end on a line that runs straight back into the first frame.
9. **Real evidence:** archive photos, maps and documents (public domain), not just text.
10. **Accuracy:** no invented quotes or numbers, and say so when historians disagree. Trust keeps people subscribed.

## 3. Style packs (long videos: 4-6 styles, one per chapter)
| Pack | Order | Best topics |
|---|---|---|
| **Investigation** (preview video) | caption-news → archive-lighttable → briefing-board → satellite-briefing → silent-film → countdown | Revolutions, protests, "how X beat Y" |
| **Time Machine** | newsreel → kino-montage → archive-lighttable → city-symphony → countdown | Inventions, everyday things |
| **Mystery File** | spy-titles → visual-investigation → halftone-dossier → lidar-survey → telestrator → caption-news | Lost cities, unsolved history |

| **Ancient Asia** | ink-wash → ukiyoe* → papercut-red* → shadow-puppet* → caption-news | China, Japan, Silk Road |
| **Retro Arcade** | ascii-crt → pixel-rpg* → cel-anime-80s* → hd-2d* → game-show | Wars, empires and rivalries told as a game |
| **Golden Age** | art-deco* → newsreel → urban-sketch* → silkscreen-poster* → countdown | The 1920s-50s, cities, brands |

\* AI-built (Claude designs it for each video, so it's slower). Use one or two of these per long video, as a "wow" chapter.

## 4. Shorts: 2 styles (a hook style, then a story style)
- caption-news → visual-investigation ("The empire beaten by salt")
- countdown → archive-lighttable ("3 crazy facts about…")
- telestrator → satellite-briefing (map stories)
- spy-titles → briefing-board (mysteries)
- ukiyoe*, papercut-red* or shadow-puppet* → caption-news captions (thumb-stopping illustrated look)

Shorts rules: under 50 s, the first word on screen at frame 1, a cut every 1.5-3 s, and an ending that loops back to the start.

## 5. Daily schedule (2 long + 4 shorts)
- Long 1: an "Investigation" or "Mystery File" pack (highest retention).
- Long 2: rotate the other packs, so the channel keeps one recognisable look per series.
- Shorts 1-2: cut from Long 1's best two moments, in 2 different style pairs.
- Shorts 3-4: from Long 2.
